# Sample AI & Technology Intelligence Report

**Project:** Daily AI & Technology Intelligence Report  
**Automation Platform:** n8n  
**AI Provider:** OpenRouter  
**Frequency:** Daily at 8:00 AM IST  
**Delivery:** Email  

---

## 📊 Executive Summary

This report is automatically generated by an n8n workflow that collects recent AI and technology news from multiple RSS feeds.

The workflow processes the collected articles by cleaning the data, removing duplicate articles, sorting them by publication date, and sending the selected news to an AI model for analysis.

The final report is generated in a structured format and delivered through email.
